Hi, I'm Jet. I lead Product Security programs through the messy parts — the parts where the threat model changes faster than the policy can catch up. I've spent three decades in software — the last decade in security — and I've found that the teams who win aren't the ones with the most controls. They're the ones who built security into the way the agents work in the first place.

Distinguished Engineer at GEICO today. Conference speaker, OWASP contributor, host of a 62-episode AppSec podcast. Off the clock you'll find me overlanding the Tundra, building extracts for cocktails, or up on HF working a contact 4,000 miles away.
